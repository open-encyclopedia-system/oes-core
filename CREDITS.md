## Predecessor Software

### Open Encyclopedia System (OES), 2016–2020

This plugin builds on the **Open Encyclopedia System (OES)**, a standardized
platform for creating, publishing, and maintaining scholarly online
encyclopedias in the humanities and social sciences. OES was developed as
part of the DFG-funded project [“From 1914-1918-online to the Open
Encyclopedia
System”](https://www.open-encyclopedia-system.org/ueber_uns/eigene-projekte/dfg-oes.html)
(2016–2020) and implemented as a WordPress plugin with accompanying themes.
Proof of concept was provided by
[Compendium heroicum](https://www.compendium-heroicum.de/) (SFB 948,
University of Freiburg), the
[Compendium of German-Greek
Entanglements](https://comdeg.eu/) (CeMoG, Freie Universität Berlin), and
[1914-1918-Online](https://encyclopedia.1914-1918-online.net/) (FMI, Freie
Universität Berlin).

Since October 2020, the OES code has been available under a **GPLv2
license** on [GitHub](https://github.com/open-encyclopedia-system/) for use
and extension by third parties.

**Project lead:** Prof. Nicolas Apostolopoulos
**Implementation of the OES prototype:** Ilker Egilmez

### 1914-1918-online: International Encyclopedia of the First World War, 2011–2015

The conceptual foundations for OES trace back to the DFG-funded project
[“1914-1918-online: International Encyclopedia of the First World
War.”](https://encyclopedia.1914-1918-online.net/) Published in 2014, it was
one of the first encyclopedias to be published entirely digitally and
continues to set standards for the structure, design, and interlinking of
encyclopedic content online.
The custom-built software solution for this project was developed by CeDiS
and was later replaced, in the follow-up project, by the standardized OES
solution.

**Project lead:** Prof. Oliver Janz, Dr. Jennifer Willenberg
**Software development (CeDiS):** Prof. Nicolas Apostolopoulos (PI), Ilker Egilmez

footnotes). See `includes/popup` for the modified implementation.
